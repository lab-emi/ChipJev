BEHIND THE DEMO

Fast decisions.
Measured circuits.

ChipJev combines a System One decision model, joint topology and sizing search, and open-source EDA.

Explore the research and results ↗
01 / DECIDE

Your prompt becomes typed decisions.

Every click runs the fine-tuned Laya model. Its probabilities guide the search over the circuit grammar, constrained to the number of stages in your selected prompt. The actual device and inference time are shown above.

02 / DESIGN

Search topology and sizing together.

Laya and Bayesian search use CUDA when the GPU backend is connected. Eight ngspice CPU workers measure real candidate circuits. This bounded demo stops at the first strictly verified design, or reports that its search budget was exhausted.

03 / VERIFY

Open the circuit. Inspect the evidence.

Download the current run’s Laya decisions, search trace, wired schematic, SPICE netlist and measurements. Every run starts from the selected prompt. The idle preview is a reference capture; live results come from new computations.
