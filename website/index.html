BEHIND THE DEMO

Fast decisions.
Measured circuits.

ChipJev combines a System One decision model, joint topology and sizing search, and open-source EDA.

Explore the research and results ↗
01 / DECIDE

A typed prior, in milliseconds.

The Laya decision model answers structured design questions. Its answers guide a search across a grammar of 4,562 valid circuit topologies.

02 / DESIGN

Search topology and sizing together.

GPU-accelerated Bayesian search evaluates candidates with parallel ngspice workers. Only circuits that pass strict qualification count.

03 / VERIFY

Open the circuit. Inspect the evidence.

This demo uses cmota_n+inv_cas+miller: the largest by MOSFET count among the study’s 60 qualified ChipJev final designs. Each run rebuilds and simulates this published design, with assembly paced for visibility. It does not rerun the topology search.
