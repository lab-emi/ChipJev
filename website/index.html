Noise and supply-response measurements

Nominal block verification. Supply, gate-bias sources and the 100 pF load are external. The layout loop compares real PDK geometry, extracted RC and fixed-condition simulations. Noise and supply measurements use an explicit finite-impedance fixture; no noise limit is assumed. Dimensions are snapped to the PDK grid. DRC and LVS do not establish fabrication signoff or PVT yield.

Plots and measurements come from the current ngspice run.

01 / DECIDE

Your prompt becomes typed decisions.

Every click runs the fine-tuned Laya model. Its probabilities guide the search over the circuit grammar, constrained to the number of stages in your selected prompt. The actual device and inference time are shown above.

02 / DESIGN

Search topology and sizing together.

Laya and Bayesian search use CUDA when the GPU backend is connected. Eight ngspice CPU workers measure real candidate circuits. Gain and OTA prompts reserve modest search margins. Strict candidates undergo real PEX screening before the search accepts them. The layout loop then compares matched placement, packing and supply options at one fixed input bias; every attempt is recorded.

03 / VERIFY

From schematic to verified physical layout.

Download the current run’s Laya decisions, search trace, wired schematic, Magic layout, GDSII, extracted RC netlist and both sets of measurements. Every run starts from the selected prompt. The idle preview is a reference capture; live results come from new computations.
